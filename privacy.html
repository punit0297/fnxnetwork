When you join our server or visit our store, we may collect minimal data including:

2. How We Use Your Data

Your information is used strictly for the following purposes:

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. We never sell, trade, or leak your personal information to third-party companies.

Our website may use cookies to improve your browsing experience, such as remembering your login status for the official store.

FNX NETWORK reserves the right to update this policy. We recommend players check this page periodically for any changes.
